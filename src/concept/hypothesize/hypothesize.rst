====================
Hypothesize Measures
====================

The Ufil framework provides several hypothesize methods for measurement-to-track similarity comparison. Next to well known measures such as the Euclidean distance, Mahalanobis distance, Interseciton over Union (IoU), Ufil also includes more experimental approaches such as a closed form Wasserstein disatnce.
